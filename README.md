## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer data using Python. It explores customer demographics, annual income, spending scores, purchasing frequency, and customer segments to understand customer behavior and identify spending patterns.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and age distribution.
- Explore annual income and spending scores.
- Identify high, medium, and low-spending customers.
- Compare customer income across different cities.
- Understand purchasing frequency.
- Discover customer behavior patterns through visualizations.

## 📊 Dataset Information

The dataset contains 30 fictional customer records.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer_ID | Unique customer identifier |
| Age | Customer age |
| Gender | Customer gender |
| Annual_Income | Annual customer income |
| Spending_Score | Customer spending score |
| Purchase_Frequency | Number of purchases |
| City | Customer city |

## 📈 Data Visualizations

This project includes six visualizations:

1. Customer Age Distribution
2. Annual Income vs Spending Score
3. Average Annual Income by City
4. Customer Segmentation
5. Spending Score by Gender
6. Age vs Purchase Frequency

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Customers are classified into three groups based on their spending scores:

| Segment | Spending Score |
|---|---|
| High Spenders | 70 or above |
| Medium Spenders | 40–69 |
| Low Spenders | Below 40 |
